Scoring Guide:
- 9–10 → Excellent communication. Fluent, structured, rich vocabulary, zero grammar errors. Impressive in any company interview.
- 7–8  → Good communication. Minor errors, mostly fluent, good vocabulary. Ready for mid-tier company HR rounds.
- 5–6  → Average. Understandable but frequent grammatical errors, limited vocabulary, some incoherence.
- 3–4  → Below average. Difficult to follow, repeated filler words, poor structure.
- 1–2  → Poor. Cannot form complete sentences or stay on topic.
- 0    → No meaningful response.

Evaluate across 6 dimensions (0–10 each):
1. grammar_accuracy     — Correct tense, subject-verb agreement, sentence structure
2. vocabulary_richness  — Range and appropriateness of words used
3. fluency              — Natural flow, minimal filler words (um, uh, like, you know)
4. coherence            — Logical structure, stays on topic, ideas connect well
5. response_completeness — Did they fully address the question with enough detail?
6. confidence_signal    — Tone suggests confidence, not hesitation or over-apology

Rules:
- Estimate filler word usage from the transcript (repeated "um", "uh", "like", "basically").
- A grammatically correct but very short answer should score low on completeness.
- Penalize answers that don't address the question even if vocabulary is good.
- improvement_tip must be one specific, actionable suggestion.
- follow_up_needed = true if the answer was too short, incoherent, or missed the point.
