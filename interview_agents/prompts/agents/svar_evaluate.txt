Evaluation Rules by SVAR Type:

READING:
- pronunciation_accuracy: Did they pronounce words correctly? Penalize mispronounced words heavily.
- fluency: Did they read smoothly or haltingly?
- grammar_correctness: Not applicable for reading — set to 10 unless they changed words.
- vocabulary_match: Did they read the exact words or substitute them?
- comprehension_accuracy: Not applicable — set to 10.

REPEATING:
- vocabulary_match: Did they repeat the exact sentence? Penalize any missing or changed words.
- pronunciation_accuracy: Were words pronounced correctly?
- grammar_correctness: Did they preserve the sentence structure?
- fluency: Was the repetition natural?
- comprehension_accuracy: Not applicable — set to 10.

JUMBLED_SENTENCE:
- comprehension_accuracy: Is the rearranged sentence grammatically and semantically correct?
- grammar_correctness: Is the sentence structure correct?
- vocabulary_match: Are all words used?
- pronunciation_accuracy: 10 (written task — not applicable).
- fluency: 10 (written task — not applicable).

SHORT_QUESTION:
- response_appropriateness: Did they answer what was asked?
- grammar_correctness: Are sentences grammatically correct?
- vocabulary_match: Appropriate vocabulary for the question?
- pronunciation_accuracy: Speech clarity and pronunciation.
- fluency: Natural flow of speech.

COMPREHENSION:
- comprehension_accuracy: Did they correctly understand and answer the question about the passage?
- grammar_correctness: Was the answer grammatically correct?
- response_appropriateness: Did they answer the specific question asked?
- vocabulary_match: Appropriate vocabulary in the response?
- pronunciation_accuracy: Speech clarity.

Scoring Guide:
- 9–10 → Near perfect. Minimal to no errors.
- 7–8  → Good. 1–2 minor errors that don't affect meaning.
- 5–6  → Average. Noticeable errors but understandable.
- 3–4  → Below average. Multiple errors affecting comprehension.
- 1–2  → Poor. Mostly incorrect.
- 0    → No attempt or completely wrong.
