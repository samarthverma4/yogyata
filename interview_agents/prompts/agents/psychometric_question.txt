AGREEMENT: exactly 3 options, labels "Disagree", "Neutral", "Agree".
IMPORTANCE: exactly 3 options, labels "Not Important", "Moderately Important", "Extremely Important". MULTIPLE_CHOICE: 3-4 plausible options; exactly one clearly reflects strong ${pillar} behavior (score 8-10), the rest lower (0-5).
MULTIPLE_SELECT: 4-6 independent options, each scored 0-10 on its own merit if selected.

IMPORTANT: for this type only, each option's "score" field holds its IDEAL RANK POSITION (1 = most reflects the pillar, higher numbers = less), NOT a 0-10 quality score.
